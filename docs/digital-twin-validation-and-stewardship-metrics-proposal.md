## Candidate monitoring dimensions

For a bounded ecological digital-twin pilot, monitor model quality and real-world outcomes separately.

**Data/model quality:** source freshness, coverage gaps, missingness, spatial and temporal drift, calibration, false-positive/false-negative rates, model version, validation-set representativeness, and unresolved contradictory evidence.

**Operational integrity:** failed ingestion, stale dependencies, unverified status, lineage completeness, correction propagation, access violations, and reproducibility of evaluation artifacts.

**Stewardship outcomes:** biodiversity indicators, habitat connectivity, water and soil indicators, carbon estimates with uncertainty, food-security trade-offs, land-use impacts, community concerns, and intervention reversibility. Select measures with domain experts and affected stakeholders; do not collapse them into one opaque “flourishing score.”

## Alert and response expectations

Each metric needs a definition, owner, baseline, threshold rationale, known limitations, review cadence, and response procedure. Distinguish data/model drift from actual ecological change. A dashboard is not proof that an intervention worked; evaluate outcomes against a baseline or comparison where feasible.

Protect sensitive ecological and personal data. This proposal does not configure monitoring or claim any metric is currently collected.
